One switch turns the screen gray, as warm as the phone allows, and dim: Android's own colour correction, Night Light and extra dim driven together, and put back as they were when you switch off. The switch lives in the quick settings, in a home-screen widget and in the app. It needs a permission given once from a computer (one adb command, shown in the app); no root. Android does not let an app remove blue entirely, so the tint stops at the phone's warmest setting. No network permission, no account, nothing collected.
