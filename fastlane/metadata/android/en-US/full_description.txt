One switch turns the screen gray, as warm as the phone allows, and dim: Android's own colour correction, Night Light and extra dim driven together, and put back as they were when you switch off. The switch lives in the quick settings, in a home-screen widget and in the app; a schedule can turn it on at a time or at sunset and off in the morning. The full filter needs a permission given once from a computer (one adb command, shown in the app); no root. Without it, the app lays a warm, dimmed veil over the apps instead, without grayscale. Android does not let an app remove blue entirely, so the tint stops at the phone's warmest setting. No network permission, no account, nothing collected.
